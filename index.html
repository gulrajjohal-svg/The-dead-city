DEAD CITY: TEN NIGHTS

The machines wake when the sun goes down. Survive and destroy every one for 10 nights.

Every building has a door. Scavenge crates and medkits by day, fight at night.

Machines can't walk through walls and lasers stop at walls, so buildings are your shelter. The city loops forever in every direction.

PAUSED · SURVIVAL GUIDE

Click anywhere or press Esc to resume.

Controls: WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Mouse look · Click attack (hold for chainsaw and rifle) · 1-9 or mouse wheel switch weapon · E open chest · H use medpack · Esc pause.

Chests (70% of buildings): White = Medical. Only 5 exist in the whole city and each holds medpacks and sometimes a defibrillator that revives you once, and each has a tall white light beam. Gold = Weapons (sword, spear, chainsaw, and a rare RPG). Blue = Guns (pistol, shotgun, rifle, bow and arrows). Green = Ammo crate with random ammo.

Ammo: 9mm for the pistol · shells for the shotgun · rifle rounds for the rifle · arrows for the bow · rockets for the RPG (explosion hurts you too if you are close). Guns are useless without ammo.

Machines: Drone: flies, and shuts down after 1 minute of attacking you. Walker: blocked by walls. Cyborg (half human, half robot, from night 2): quick and shoots. Spider: climbs over walls. Tank: slow with heavy lasers. Colossus (nights 5, 7, 9) and the Overlord (night 10): huge bosses. Bomber (night 3+): rolls at you and explodes. Swarm drones (night 3+): tiny and come in threes. Sniper (night 4+): long range, hits hard, keeps its distance. Juggernaut (night 5+): slow, armoured, heavy. No machine can walk through walls and lasers stop at walls. The map loops forever, so walk far enough and the city repeats. Beat the game to unlock Easy, Hard, Insane and Endless modes.

Tips: Scavenge by day, fight at night. Walls block most lasers. Crates give scrap, which upgrades your fists. Kills give scrap and health. Destroy the Overlord on night 10 to win.

Floors and stairs: taller buildings have up to 3 floors linked by stairs. Chests and furniture are upstairs too, and furniture is solid downstairs.

Car parts: 5 car parts are hidden in the city (tall orange light beams): engine, wheel, battery, headlight, steering wheel. Find all 5 to summon the Junkyard King. Beat it to win the Railgun, which pierces every machine in a line.

Bosses: Spider Queen (night 4), Colossus (5, 8), Behemoth (6, 9), Sky Fortress (7), Overlord (10).
